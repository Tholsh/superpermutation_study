# Historical provenance and what is NOT fully replayable

This ZIP prioritizes **genuinely recovered source** and independently checked key results, especially the RLLB certificate. It is not an assertion that every program ever executed in earlier conversations survived container/session resets.

**Fully replayed with this bundle:** PacketKraft native local tests, n12 literal connector trie tests, 39 exhaustive RLLB toy cases, n12 Q210 saved-price certificate regeneration, all 256 n13 kernel profiles, independent full n13 native Q/D/T/monodromy checks, both n11 SPRA 529/527 decompression and full 11! bitmap coverage checks. Each wrapper is newly authored but runs archived tools untouched, except a portable temporary copy of the n13 profile script's hard-coded path.

**Sources provided, but giant replay deferred:** n12 522736406 witness (original SPRA + selection + plan + recorded literal verification), n13 6747834425 and 6747788255 SPRA witnesses (full ~6.7e9 symbol decode/audit is not run). The current SPRA witness alone does not encode the native selected row choices. Full n13 native paths/checks exist for the archived n13 experiments, not necessarily for the newest SPRA witness.

**Missing historical run intermediates:** The original n11 43,930,529 global 50-unit state catalogue `allfiber_meta.json`, the exact winning `best_block_order.tsv`, original `block_order_dp_long.cpp`, `materialize_order.py`, and the later 43,930,527 core-free global placement code/TSV were not available as maintained original files in the accessible saved archives. The historical algorithms are documented in `documents/how_n11_43930529_was_found.md` and `documents/how_n11_43930527_was_found.md` and the *complete native 529 selection* and original 529/527 SPRA words are supplied. These facts do not recreate the exact sequence of optimization moves. Do not claim to replay the historical search from scratch on the basis of word validity alone.

**Model limitations:** The archived n12/n13 global RLLB is a rigorous restricted one-intact-weight-3-cut model. It is not an unrestricted certificate including weight-2 cuts, split trails, repeated clean windows, or all full-word arrangement freedoms. Price-feature projections, spectral/Kraft pressure and local connector heuristics are not globally valid lower bounds without an independently stated proof.

**Upstream programs and licensing:** The recovered PacketKraft archive includes `LICENSE-APACHE-2.0.txt` and bundled SPRA algebra. Other recovered archives may carry upstream dependencies/license obligations not repeated here. Original archive names and SHA-256 hashes are given in `ORIGINAL_ARCHIVES.json`. For operational completeness, obtain updates/licenses directly from their source repositories before redistribution.
