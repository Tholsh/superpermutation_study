The file 7_5907_FFC_4SYMM.txt contains 762 superpermutations for n=7 of length 5907.

I believe this set is a complete enumeration of all solutions of this length that are rooted in the initial 4-cycle of the standard n=7 superpermutation, and that possess fourfold symmetry under the group generated by the two automorphisms:

(1 3) and reverse
(2 4)(5 6) and reverse

i.e. swapping the digits 1 and 3 and reversing the permutation, and
swapping the digits 2 and 4, and also the digits 5 and 6, and reversing the permutation.

These generators give rise to a group of order 4, whose only other nontrivial element is their product:

(1 3)(2 4)(5 6).
