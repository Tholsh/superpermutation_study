# Personal research notes: N=13

We stopped treating the inherited record selection as the only permitted starting point and tested balanced occupancy switches, compound walk changes, and alternative residual packets. The productive progress was a smaller finite packet trail count at fixed charge and a clearer account of where conservative integration loses its apparent gain.

A closed partial motif with the right monodromy is not automatically a complete balanced selection. Repeating an entire native completion has a real cost; the archived 1,859-symbol figure concerns full duplicate completion, not every possible short detour. Whole-walk demotion repairs closure but may introduce far too many trails.

The 56 tied minimum-floor packets have identical simple relaxed overlap counts. The different local greedy path covers therefore motivate further phase-coupled selection and arrangement work; they do not identify an optimal mask. A bound below a comparison word leaves room for improvement but is not an upper bound on an achieved word.

Use Pantone's **transport** only for its defined map. The original A/B/H interiors contain visible lengths that do not have the ordinary full/short transport. The finite `(7,3)` port-cycle pattern at order 13 is evidence for an attempted extension, not proof that the N=11 construction has been lifted generally. File names containing `Lift` describe experiments, not established lift results.
