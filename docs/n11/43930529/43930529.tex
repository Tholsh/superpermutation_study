Starting from the $43{,}930{,}543$-symbol precursor, we constructed a
$43{,}930{,}529$-symbol superpermutation: fourteen fewer symbols than the
precursor and five fewer than Halfar's then-published $43{,}930{,}534$.
The Almanac records this as \href{https://superpermutations.com/w/W-0131}{W-0131}.
The advance changes the selected fiber realizations and their arrangement
jointly; it is not a claim of minimum length or a new all-order construction.

\section*{Definitions and inherited construction}
We use Pantone's slices and completion~\cite{pantone}, with the glossary's
selection/arrangement distinction~\cite{glossary}. Put $K=n-2$.
A native selection chooses a row, or no row, in each of the $(K-1)!$
\emph{2-loops}. A row has starting ordering $x$ and \emph{visible length}
$v$; full and short rows have $v=K$ and $v=K-2$. The native-file value
$v=0$ denotes a loop without a row. Its charge is not included in
$Q=\sum_{v>0}(K-v)$.
Completion gives closed trails on $n$ symbols, with cyclic spelling sum
\[
F_3(n)+Q,\qquad F_3(n)=n!+(n-1)!+(n-2)!.
\]
Closed walks of selected rows and closed trails of the completion are
different objects. For general visible lengths, a walk's completed trails
are counted by cycles of its port-monodromy permutation.

At $n=11$, the complete selection has $8!=40{,}320$ native 2-loops.
The precursor exposes 48 complete $(7,2)$ fiber blocks and two large
literal core sections. A block has 56 native 2-loops and covers 55,440
assigned eleven-symbol permutations. These 48 selected fibers are not all
720 possible cyclic orders of the seven old letters.
A \emph{packet} here is a complete balanced row selection on one such block.

\section*{1. Price connections as well as charge}
We began with Halfar's packet ($Q=38$, 14 loops without a row, $T=16$
completed trails) and retained alternatives including $Q=22,T=30$ and
$Q=29,T=24$. Local connector tries, prefix conflicts, and Kraft mass
made endpoint capacity visible, but did not prove that favorable local
connectors could coexist in a full arrangement. Their role was diagnostic.

Instead of freezing a preferred packet before arrangement, we retained
complete realizations for every target fiber. A unit state records its
source template, cyclic relabeling of the seven old letters, optional
exchange of 7 and 8, actual length, and ten-symbol endpoint words.
Relabeling preserves the target fiber's assigned permutation support.
The two cores remain literal pieces.

\section*{2. Reopen all state choices when changing order}
For a proposed order $u_1,\ldots,u_{50}$, let $w_i(s)$ be a legal literal
state of unit $u_i$. Write $\operatorname{ov}(a,b)$ for the largest equal
suffix/prefix overlap of length zero through ten. The attainable objective is
\[
L=\sum_{i=1}^{50}|w_i(s_i)|-
\sum_{i=2}^{50}\operatorname{ov}\bigl(w_{i-1}(s_{i-1}),w_i(s_i)\bigr).
\]
The opening/template DP is
\[
D_1(s)=|w_1(s)|,\qquad
D_i(s)=|w_i(s)|+\min_t\left[D_{i-1}(t)-\operatorname{ov}(w_{i-1}(t),w_i(s))\right].
\]
Backpointers materialize the chosen states. Every trial order is evaluated
with its states chosen again, not with phases inherited from the previous
order. The DP is exact for a fixed order and finite state library; searching
orders is heuristic. The historical 529 account kept the core units
anchored in its search. The newly supplied replacement search explicitly
anchors the cores in the slots of its supplied input order.

Overlaps of at most $n-1=10$ preserve every complete eleven-symbol window
inside each retained unit. Extra boundary windows may change; preserving
occupation support does not mean preserving all occurrence multiplicities.

\section*{4. Current RLLB-first chain and exact replay}
The first computational stage in \texttt{REPRODUCE.md} builds and runs
\path{search/rllb.cpp} as \path{build/rllb}, on the complete saved 529
selection. The current run regenerates $Q=20{,}089$, 1,364 trails,
and 382,969 legal completion ports. Twenty depth-6/7 price updates give
restricted $L\geq43{,}930{,}158$; this does not construct the record.
This modern stage is not attributed retroactively to the original local
Kraft diagnostic.

The current RLLB state space opens each completed trail once at weight 3,
with endpoint width $h=n-3$ and overlaps at most $h-1$. The whole-fiber DP
above uses different literal units and overlaps up to ten. A rejection in
one restricted model must not be extended to unrestricted openings or splits.

The saved 543 precursor, fiber manifest, and recovered 529 order now
reconstruct the word without reading the 529 witness. The current receipt
checks its literal hash and its canonical identity against SPRA and W-0131.
The supplied search program calls itself a new replacement for the lost
historical optimizer; the exact discovery move sequence remains distinct
from exact saved-plan replay.

\section*{5. Coverage, attribution, and subsequent work}
The recorded independent scan found all $11!=39{,}916{,}800$ permutations,
with 19,518 repeated clean occurrences. The current default replay verifies
exact word identity; a fresh factorial scan is an explicit option, not
silently claimed as having been run again.
Pantone supplied the construction, Halfar the generalized selection and
monodromy machinery, and this work the connection-aware packet selection
and placement experiment, with AI-assisted implementation and search.
Later W-0132 and Pantone's W-0133 are separate results~\cite{almanac}.
