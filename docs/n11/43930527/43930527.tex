The next experiment shortened the $43{,}930{,}529$ word to
$43{,}930{,}527$, recorded as
\href{https://superpermutations.com/w/W-0132}{W-0132}.
It is two symbols shorter than W-0131 and seven shorter than Halfar's
then-published $43{,}930{,}534$ benchmark. The change removes a placement
restriction and reoptimizes available literal unit states.

\section*{Definitions and the retained units}
We retain Pantone's construction and the selection/arrangement distinction
of~\cite{pantone,glossary}. A completed \emph{closed trail} is not the
Reduced Alphabet's \emph{course}, which is one clean rotation edge~\cite{spra}.
The arrangement has 48 complete $(7,2)$ fiber units and two large literal
cores. A unit state specifies a complete template, old-letter relabeling
phase, optional 7/8 exchange, length, and ten-symbol endpoint words.
Whole-unit relabeling preserves assigned permutation support.

\section*{1. The search restriction that changed}
The historical 529 account fixed the core positions in its search.
The 527 search allowed a core to move as an intact literal unit, or,
equivalently, allowed a fiber section to cross it. It did not edit the
core's internal literal content.

The recovered orders identify the decisive section as unit IDs
$34,38,39,40,41$. Those five consecutive fibers move from after the first
core to before it. The first core shifts from zero-based position 37 to 42;
the second remains at position 48. Template and relabeling choices are
reselected together with the new order.

\section*{2. Fixed-order opening/template optimization}
For an order $u_1,\ldots,u_{50}$ and state choices $s_i$, define
\[
\operatorname{ov}(a,b)=\max\{0\leq k\leq10:
\operatorname{suffix}_k(a)=\operatorname{prefix}_k(b)\}.
\]
The objective and opening DP are
\[
L=\sum_i|w_i(s_i)|-\sum_{i>1}\operatorname{ov}(w_{i-1}(s_{i-1}),w_i(s_i)),
\]
\[
D_1(s)=|w_1(s)|,\qquad
D_i(s)=|w_i(s)|+\min_t[D_{i-1}(t)-\operatorname{ov}(w_{i-1}(t),w_i(s))].
\]
Every proposed placement is scored with fresh state optimization. This is
exact for the fixed order and enumerated library; it does not solve the
unrestricted order problem. The overlap cap preserves complete internal
length-eleven windows of each unit.

\section*{3. Exact accounting from both recovered plans}
The supplied 529 plan and newly recovered 527 plan give
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lrrr}\toprule
 &529 plan&527 plan&Change\\\midrule
Sum of whole-unit lengths&43,930,683&43,930,685&$+2$\\
Sum of exact literal overlaps&154&158&$+4$\\\midrule
Emitted word length&43,930,529&43,930,527&$-2$\\\bottomrule
\end{tabular}\end{center}
Consequently
\[
\boxed{\Delta L=\Delta\!\sum |w_i|-\Delta\!\sum\operatorname{ov}=2-4=-2.}
\]
This is more precise than assigning the gain exclusively to overlap:
the selected piece lengths also change. The recorded native 529 selection
must not automatically be called the final 527 selection.

\section*{4. Reproducibility and its scope}
The first computational chain runs the new \path{search/rllb.cpp}, built
as \path{build/rllb}. Its input is explicitly the complete \emph{529
predecessor selection}, used as a control; no independently recovered final
527 native selection is claimed. The same $Q=20{,}089$, 1,364-trail graph
therefore gives the same restricted screening bound as the 529 control.
The score is not presented as the objective of the historical free-core search.

The second chain assembles 527 from the original 543 precursor, its fiber
manifest, and the separately recovered 527 order. The recovery matches
complete relabeled source pieces and unchanged cores to the known witness,
with every unit used once. The saved plan then replays without reading that
witness. Its literal SHA-256 is
\begin{center}\small
\path{8b78860eebb7d25ffbf1147b6609d5c156e2fed2e0a16c5a23ad949cc0b5825c}.
\end{center}
Canonical relabeling matches the pinned SPRA corpus and W-0132.
The recovery program is provided and writes only under \path{build/}.
It is a new witness-derived recovery tool, not the missing historical
free-core optimizer or its random-state log.

The recorded independent scan covers all $39{,}916{,}800$ permutations,
with 19,518 repeated clean occurrences. This session's default replay
checks exact word identity; a new coverage scan is optional.

\section*{5. Attribution and continuation}
The construction is Pantone's and the native selection machinery builds
on Halfar's work. The contribution here is the core-unpinned,
connection-aware arrangement change, with AI-assisted exploration.
Pantone subsequently reached $43{,}930{,}517$ (W-0133) using expanded
fiber geometries and a connected Euler-flow order model~\cite{almanac}.
That later improvement is credited separately, and no optimality of 527
is inferred from unsuccessful additional local search.
