\section{Starting-phase optimization}

Starting from the length-$43{,}930{,}628$ word in PR~\#5, we obtained
a verified $11$-symbol superpermutation of length $43{,}930{,}624$.

We retained Jay Pantone's $2{,}800$ closed trails, their cyclic
transition sequences, and their order in the source word.
We changed only their starting phases.

A starting phase specifies a position in the periodic spelling
of a closed trail, including which constituent cycle is entered
and the position within that cycle. From this position, we follow
the fixed cyclic spelling until every permutation assigned to
the trail has appeared as a complete window. This determines
the shortest coverage-preserving opening for that position.
The portion preceding the chosen start is encountered after
wrapping around; it is not discarded.

For trail $i$, let $c_i$ be its period length and $P_i$ its set
of admitted starting positions. For $p\in P_i$, define:
\[
h_i(p)=\text{opened length}-c_i,
\]
and let $F_i(p)$ and $T_i(p)$ be its first and last
$11$-symbol windows. Let $o(a,b)$ denote the maximal directed
suffix--prefix overlap between $a$ and $b$.

The assembled length is
\[
L=
\sum_{i=1}^{2800}c_i
+\sum_{i=1}^{2800}h_i(p_i)
-\sum_{i=1}^{2799}
o\!\left(T_i(p_i),F_{i+1}(p_{i+1})\right).
\]
We minimize this exactly by dynamic programming:
\[
D_1(p)=h_1(p),
\qquad
D_{i+1}(q)=h_{i+1}(q)
+\min_{p\in P_i}
\left[D_i(p)-o\!\left(T_i(p),F_{i+1}(q)\right)\right].
\]
Here $D_i(p)$ is the minimum accumulated opening adjustment
minus overlaps for the first $i$ trails, ending at opening $p$.

We admitted every permutation-window start assigned to its
trail: $39{,}916{,}800$ assigned occurrences plus $18{,}816$
additional occurrences, giving $39{,}935{,}616$ candidates.
One opening was selected per trail. Exact suffix--prefix
buckets evaluated the recurrence without constructing dense
all-pairs overlap matrices.

Each opening preserves its assigned permutation set, and exact
overlap joins preserve the opened words as contiguous substrings.
The assigned sets partition $S_{11}$, so coverage is retained.

The result uses nine fewer opening characters but removes five
fewer characters through overlap, saving four characters overall.
Three independent full-word checkers confirmed all $11!$
permutations, with none missing.

This is a joint starting-phase optimization of the inherited
construction, not a relocation of trails or a new standalone
all-$n$ construction.
